
## Account (Server) — Represents a company
  - Id: IntegerType
  - IsActive: BooleanType * — Indicates that the record is active
  - Company: TextType(100) * — Company name
  - Code: TextType(10) * — Account unique code
  - Contact: TextType(100) * — Main contact inside the company
  - CountryId: ? [del:Protect] — Company country
  - Address: TextType(250) — Company address
  - City: TextType(50) — Company city
  - PostalCode: TextType(50) — Company postal code
  - Phone: PhoneNumberType — Company phone number
  - Email: EmailType — Company email
  - CreatedBy: →user * [del:Protect] — User that created the record
  - CreatedOn: DateTimeType * — Timestamp when the record was created
  - UpdatedBy: →user [del:Protect] — Last user that updated the record
  - UpdatedOn: DateTimeType — Timestamp when the record was updated by the last time
