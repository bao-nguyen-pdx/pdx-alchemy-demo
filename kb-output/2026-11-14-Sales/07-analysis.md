## 1. Application summary

**Sales** is an OutSystems 11 Traditional Web CRM. It helps a sales team keep track of the companies, contacts and opportunities they own, and gives them a weighted sales forecast.

There are two business roles:
- An **AccountManager** works only on the companies assigned to them. Each company has one account manager (`AccountManager` entity). They also see the contacts, opportunities, tasks and notes for those companies.
- A **SalesManager** sees every company. They set each account manager's quarter quota (`AccountManager_SetupQuotas`) and get a team dashboard (`Dashboard_SalesManager`).

The main lifecycle is the **opportunity pipeline**:
- An opportunity is created against a company.
- It moves through probability stages: 10 %, 20 %, 40 %, 70 %, 80 %, 90 %.
- It closes as **Won** (100 %) or **Lost** (0 %).
- Every change is written to the opportunity's history.

The second lifecycle is **looking after companies and contacts**: create, edit, assign or reassign the account manager, import contacts, and delete. Each step goes into the company or contact history, along with notes and follow-up tasks.

Where the data lives:
- The `Customers` service holds companies, contacts and their histories.
- The `Employees` service holds the employee directory, which is used for user info.
- Three `*SampleData` modules load demo data and demo users when the app is published.

## 2. Business rules

How to read the "Enforced where" column:
- **Server** means the rule is checked in a server action, in a screen Preparation (which runs on the server), or by the data model.
- **UI-only** means the rule is checked only in a screen action or a widget condition. The action that actually saves the data does not check it again.
